# Cradle Mod — Ideas & Brainstorms

> Future ideas and brainstorms — not committed to, just captured for reference

---

## Gameplay Ideas

- **Craftable Badges** — Appear on character model and next to nametag. Show path/rank
- **Forging with Anvil + Hammer** — Soulsmith system for crafting sacred materials
- **Half-Silver Weapons** — Special weapons effective against Remnants and spirits
- **Foundation Pure Cycling** — Before Copper, only gain XP from cycling yourself (pure madra, like Hollow King). At Copper, unlock aura cycling
- **Sacred Beast Contracts** — Bond with a sacred beast, share cores, beast fights alongside you, your eyes change to match bonded beast
- **Soulspace Inventory** — Personal spiritual storage unlocked at Underlord (lore-accurate ender chest)

## Lore Accuracy Improvements

- **Iron Body Rework** — Change from crystal pickup to cycling-based system with three quality tiers that affect future growth ceiling
- **Jade Spiral Core** — Cycling mini-game or timed mechanic. Better technique = easier advancement later
- **Remnant System** — Sacred artists leave Remnants on death. Compatible ones can be absorbed for Gold advancement, incompatible ones cause damage
- **Icon Training** — Instead of instant Sage, build concept affinity through gameplay (use sword techniques thousands of times for Sword Icon)
- **Herald Remnant Fight** — Full boss fight against your own Remnant that mirrors your abilities
- **Monarch Hunger Aura** — Monarchs passively generate hunger aura, attracts Dreadbeasts, environmental effects

## World & Content Ideas

- **Dreadgod Boss Encounters** — Massive endgame bosses from Cradle lore
- **Ascension Portal** — Endgame portal leading to new dimension or server event
- **Rare Aura Types** — Death, Shadow, Dream auras that could enable entirely new Paths
- **Custom Duel Arenas** — 15 unique arena designs for all path matchup combinations
- **Aura Density Zones** — Special high-aura areas for faster cycling (like power-leveling spots)

## Quality of Life Ideas

- **Jade Senses** — See other players' madra levels, sense sacred artists through walls
- **Archlord Lore Hints** — Teaser messages about what's coming at Sage and Herald
- **Truegold Mastery Trial** — Prove mastery before entering Lord realm
- **Soulfire Body Reforging Visuals** — Each Lord advancement triggers visible upgrade (particles, glow, stat boosts)
