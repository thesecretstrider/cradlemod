# Cradle Mod — Lore Reference

> Condensed Cradle series lore that guides mod design decisions

---

## Advancement Stages

### Foundation
- Cycle pure madra only (no aura)
- Slightly strengthen body or activate simple scripts
- **Mod rule:** Only gain XP from internal cycling (like Hollow King)

### Copper
- Gain **Copper Sight** — see vital aura in the world
- Seven common aura types: Fire, Earth, Wind, Water, Force, Blood, Life
- Rare types harder to see (death, shadow, dream)
- Begin cycling vital aura into core — core takes on those aspects over time

### Iron
- Body reforged with madra. Three tiers:
  1. **Basic** — let core overflow. Grants strength/speed/durability but limits future growth
  2. **Perfect** — infuse entire body with madra first, then overflow. Stronger foundation
  3. **Advanced Perfect** — extra painful path-specific step. Grants unique abilities (regen, reflexes, strength scaling)

### Jade
- Gain **Jade Senses** — spiritual awareness, sense madra of others
- Create spiral pattern in core through cycling technique
- Quality of cycling at Jade affects all future advancement

### Gold (Lowgold / Highgold / Truegold)

**Three ways to reach Lowgold:**
1. **Absorb a Remnant** — most common, gain a Goldsign (visual cosmetic)
2. **Contract a Sacred Beast** — share cores, beast companion, eyes change color
3. **Natural Accumulation** — very slow, mainly for pure madra users

**Goldsigns** — visible cosmetic per path:
- Blackflame: flame eyes
- Endless Sword: blade-like arm details
- Cloud Hammer: crackling skin
- etc.

**Highgold** — If took a Remnant, must digest it through combat/cycling

### Lord Realm

- **Soulfire** — purified vital aura, secondary resource
- Each breakthrough requires a **Revelation** (personal question):
  - **Underlord** — "Why did I start practicing the sacred arts?"
  - **Overlord** — "Who am I today?"
  - **Archlord** — "Where am I going?"
- Soulfire washes and reforges the body at each Lord advancement

### Sage
- Manifest an **Icon** — a symbol of a greater concept
- Gain **Authority** — command reality directly through willpower
- Authority is limited to your Icon's domain (Sword Icon = cutting/sharpness, Void Icon = emptiness, etc.)

### Herald
- Merge body and spirit by fighting your own Remnant
- Can shift between physical and spirit forms
- Phase through blocks, become invisible briefly

### Monarch
- Both Sage + Herald combined
- Peak of the sacred arts
- Passively generate **hunger aura** (attracts Dreadbeasts, environmental effects)

### Ascension
- Sages, Heralds, exceptional Archlords, and Monarchs can ascend through a portal
- Endgame mechanic — leave the world behind

## Vital Aura Types

| Aura Type | Biome Examples |
|-----------|---------------|
| Fire | Nether, Deserts |
| Water | Oceans, Rivers |
| Earth | Mountains, Caves |
| Wind | High altitudes, Plains |
| Life | Forests, Jungles |
| Force | Mountains, Strongholds |
| Blood | Nether, Dark biomes |

- Rare types: Death (undead areas), Shadow, Dream — could enable future Paths
- Higher density = faster cycling speed and more madra per cycle

## Dreadbeasts

- Animals twisted by vital aura exposure
- Hostile and dangerous — not ordinary creatures
- Currently in mod: Wolves, Cows (Sheep disabled pending textures)
