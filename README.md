# Cradle Mod

A Minecraft Fabric mod inspired by Will Wight's **Cradle** book series. Progress through the sacred arts, choose your Path, unlock powerful abilities, and advance from Foundation to Monarch.

> **This mod is a work in progress.** Features are actively being developed and things may change, break, or be incomplete. Feedback and bug reports are welcome!

## About

Cradle Mod brings the progression system and combat mechanics of the Cradle universe into Minecraft. You start as a Foundation-level sacred artist, cycling madra to grow stronger, choosing one of five Paths, and advancing through 13 stages of power — all the way to Monarch.

## Features

### Sacred Arts Progression
- **13 Advancement Stages**: Foundation, Copper, Iron, Jade, Low Gold, High Gold, Truegold, Underlord, Overlord, Archlord, Sage, Herald, and Monarch
- **Madra System**: A custom resource bar (replaces the XP bar) that powers all your abilities and grows as you advance
- **Cycling**: Sit still and cycle madra to gain experience and regenerate your madra pool. Environmental bonuses reward cycling in biomes aligned with your Path
- **Willpower**: A secondary resource unlocked at Archlord, used for Sage Authority and Herald powers

### Five Paths
Choose your Path and gain unique abilities:

- **Path of Black Flame** — Aggressive fire-based combat. Burn enemies with blackflame madra, ignite targets, and create domains of ash
- **Path of the Endless Sword** — Swift precision swordsmanship. Flowing attacks, ranged sword slashes, and blade-controlled zones
- **Path of the Stellar Spear** — Forward momentum and piercing strikes. Sprint-enhanced damage, long-range piercing projectiles, and directional defense
- **Path of the Cloud Hammer** — Heavy hits and crowd control. Armored tanking, area-of-effect slams, and gravity fields that slow enemies
- **Path of the Hollow King** — Pure madra efficiency and support. Reduced costs, defensive domains, and enemy disruption

### Ability System
- **3 Ability Types per Path**: Enforcer (toggle buffs), Striker (ranged projectiles), and Ruler (area effects)
- **Charge-Up System**: Hold Striker ability keys to charge attacks — longer holds deal up to 3x damage at higher madra cost
- **Skill Tree**: Radial skill tree with branching upgrades, mastery memory, and visual progression tiers
- **6 Ability Slots**: Keybinds Z, X, C, R, F, T — unlock more slots as you advance

### Advanced Progression
- **Iron Body**: Three crystal types (Bloodforged, Steelborn, Raindrop) found in the world that grant permanent body upgrades
- **Breakthrough System**: Consume Spirit Fruits and Spirit Stones to fuel advancement between stages
- **Revelation Trials**: Lord-realm breakthroughs (Underlord, Overlord, Archlord) require answering personal revelations
- **Sage Authority**: At Sage, use the power of Authority to Stop or Kill enemies with willpower
- **Herald Spirit Shift**: Phase between physical and spirit forms
- **Monarch Power**: Combine Sage and Herald abilities at the peak of sacred arts
- **Icon System**: Choose your Icon at Sage — each grants different Authority commands flavored by your Path

### World Features
- **Vital Aura**: 7 aura types (Fire, Water, Earth, Wind, Life, Force, Blood) mapped to biomes. Toggle Copper Sight (H key) to see colored aura particles flowing through the world
- **Spirit Fruit Bushes**: Find Vital Fruit and Spirit Fruit bushes generating naturally in the world
- **Spirit Stones**: Mine spirit stones to fuel your advancement
- **Dreadbeast Mobs**: Wolves, cows, and sheep are retextured and hostile — these are not ordinary animals
- **Blackflame Cooking**: Black Flame sacred artists can cook food with their madra

### Combat & Multiplayer
- **Duel System**: Challenge other players to formal duels with win/loss tracking
- **Combat XP**: Earn cycling experience through combat
- **Sage vs Sage**: Authority abilities can be countered by other Sages spending willpower to resist
- **Flight**: Unlocked at different stages depending on your Path

### Quality of Life
- **Info Screen** (J key): Scrollable status panel showing all your stats, stage, madra, aura type, and more
- **Ability Slot Bar**: HUD display showing equipped abilities with cooldown overlays and charge indicators
- **Madra Bar**: Custom bar replacing the XP bar, with stage-colored display
- **Willpower Bar**: Secondary bar for Archlord+ players
- **Cycling Particles**: Visual feedback while cycling, with path-specific colors and patterns
- **Action Bar Messages**: Clean, non-intrusive notifications — no chat spam

## Controls

| Key | Action |
|-----|--------|
| G | Toggle Cycling |
| J | Open Sacred Artist Info Screen |
| K | Open Skill Tree |
| H | Toggle Copper Sight (Copper+) |
| P | Toggle Iron Body |
| Z | Ability Slot 1 (Enforcer) |
| X | Ability Slot 2 (Striker) — hold to charge |
| C | Ability Slot 3 (Ruler) |
| R / F / T | Ability Slots 4-6 |
| V | Sage Authority (tap = Stop, hold = Kill) |
| B | Herald Spirit Shift |

## Requirements

- Minecraft 1.21.11
- Fabric Loader 0.18.2+
- Fabric API 0.139.4+1.21.11
- Java 21

## Installation

1. Install [Fabric Loader](https://fabricmc.net/use/) for Minecraft 1.21.11
2. Download the [Fabric API](https://modrinth.com/mod/fabric-api) and place it in your `mods` folder
3. Download the latest Cradle Mod `.jar` from the releases and place it in your `mods` folder
4. Launch Minecraft with the Fabric profile
